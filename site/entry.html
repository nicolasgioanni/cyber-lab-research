Monitored research environment

You are entering a privately operated cyber defense lab. Visitor access is limited to reviewing published research.

Conditions of access

You may inspect published results and reproduce the experiments on your own systems. Testing this service, accessing private systems or interfering with another visitor requires the operator’s prior authorization.

Security monitoring

Security checks begin on arrival. Cloudflare may record firewall blocks in its own security events. For lab admission and content access, the private log records time, IP address, estimated country, route category, access decision and a shortened browser user-agent string. The operator can review up to seven days of activity. Older events are removed during traffic or scheduled hourly cleanup. Provider recovery copies may persist longer.

Cookies and storage

Cloudflare may set cookies for browser checks. A first-party cookie lasts up to ten minutes while you review this notice. Accepting starts a one-hour visitor session. “End visitor session” clears the lab cookie. Choosing a color theme saves that preference in your browser.
